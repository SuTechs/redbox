A tiny game. A little reset.

Red Box is a warm, tactile memory puzzle with two ways to play. Watch the boxes that glow red, remember them, then enjoy tapping all the others. Correct taps turn red too, making every little board a satisfying puzzle.

GROW YOUR MEMORY
Normal starts small and builds into a deeper challenge. Discover larger boards, more red boxes to remember and visible shuffles to follow. Each level keeps its shape while retries choose fresh reds. Replay any unlocked level without losing your progress. Levels keep going, with no countdown while you tap.

JUST PLAY AND RELAX
Choose Zen mode for a quiet break. Enjoy random shaped boards with one or two reds, no levels, no shuffles and no growing difficulty. Hearts, lotus flowers, leaves, bows, clouds, ribbons and arches make each little board a pleasure to explore. Choose Another board whenever you are ready, or take another look after a mistaken tap.

YOUR QUIET SOUNDTRACK
Choose between two original relaxing music tracks, Cloud drift and Warm keys. Soft tap sounds and little completion chimes accompany your play. Adjust music and sound separately, or mute everything with one action.

MAKE IT COMFORTABLE
Choose longer previews, reduce motion, switch haptics on or off, or add symbol cues to red boxes. Dense boards use fitted portrait and landscape layouts. Rotating your device shows the remembered boxes again before play resumes.

A PRIVATE LITTLE BREAK
No accounts, ads, tracking or in-app purchases. Your progress and preferences stay on your device. Music, fonts and game assets are bundled for offline play.
