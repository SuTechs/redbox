Meet two ways to enjoy Red Box.

GROW YOUR MEMORY
Normal begins with a small puzzle and gradually adds more red boxes, larger patterns and visible shuffles. Remember the reds, follow their movement and tap the rest. There is still no countdown while tapping.

JUST PLAY AND RELAX
Zen mode brings beautiful random shapes with no levels, no shuffling and no growing difficulty. Enjoy hearts, lotus flowers, leaves, ribbons, clouds and more. Finish a board, then choose another whenever you like.

A FRESH LITTLE LOOK
Find Zen mode right below Continue, with its new lotus-and-coral-box logo. Browse your Normal levels from the small Levels icon below Settings.

MORE COMFORTABLE BOARDS
Larger boards fit portrait and landscape layouts, with clearer controls, support for bigger text, longer previews and reduced motion. Your Normal progress and comfort preferences are preserved.

Thank you for playing. Take it one box at a time.
