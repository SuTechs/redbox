Red Box is a fully offline single-player memory puzzle. No sign-in, ads, purchases, tracking, or server access is required. Start a level, remember the boxes that briefly turn red, and tap every other box after the preview ends. Correct taps also become red. There is no time limit during tapping. Earlier unlocked levels can be replayed from Levels; red positions randomize on each attempt. Settings includes music selection, separate audio controls, reduced motion, symbol cues, and the full in-app privacy notice. All music is original and bundled.
