A tiny game. A little reset.

Red Box is a cute, calming memory game made for those moments when you want a gentle break. Watch a few boxes turn red, remember where they were, then enjoy tapping all the others.

ONE SIMPLE RULE
Remember the red. Tap the rest. Correct taps turn red too, making every little board a satisfying puzzle. Take as much time as you need while tapping. There is no countdown, score penalty, or rush.

A POCKET FULL OF SHAPES
Discover beautiful arrangements of square boxes: leaves, diamonds, ribbons, wings, and more. Levels continue endlessly, with a gentle mix of simple memories and fresh patterns. Start with one red box and gradually explore rounds with up to three.

LITTLE WINS, AT YOUR PACE
Replay any unlocked level, keep your progress, and retry whenever you like. Red positions change on each attempt, so a familiar level still feels fresh.

YOUR QUIET SOUNDTRACK
Choose between two original relaxing music tracks, Cloud drift and Warm keys. Enjoy soft tap sounds and little completion chimes. Adjust music and sound separately, or mute everything with one action.

MAKE IT COMFORTABLE
Choose a longer preview, reduce motion, switch haptics on or off, or add symbol cues to red boxes.

A PRIVATE LITTLE BREAK
No account, ads, tracking, or in-app purchases. Your progress and preferences stay on your device. Music, fonts, and game assets are bundled for offline play.
