Privacy, gently.

Red Box is made by SuTechs. We keep things simple: your game stays yours.

Effective October 2, 2026 · Red Box 1.0.0

Your game data

The Android and iOS apps do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data or gameplay information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking SDKs, purchases, or social features. The app does not request sensitive device permissions.

Stored on your device

Your highest unlocked level and preferences, including music, sounds, preview time, motion, and symbol cues, are saved locally. This information is not sent to SuTechs. To remove it, clear the app’s data or uninstall it. In the web version, clear this site’s browser storage. Device or browser backups may retain local app data according to your platform settings.

Playing on the web

The browser game is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. SuTechs does not add analytics or advertising cookies. Your gameplay and preferences remain in your browser. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information. We use support messages only to handle your request and retain them only as needed for support and any legal obligations.

Children and changes

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We will update this page if the game’s privacy practices change. Contact us with any privacy questions or requests.
