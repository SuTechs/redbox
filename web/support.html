A little help.

Questions, feedback, or a box behaving oddly? We’re here.

How to play

Watch the red boxes, let them fade, then tap every other box. Take as much time as you need. Retry freely; the red positions change on each attempt.

Audio and comfort

Open Settings to change the background music, mute music or sounds separately, or mute all audio. Reduced motion and symbol cues are available there too. In browsers, music starts after you press Play or Levels; check your device volume and browser audio permissions if it’s quiet.

Get in touch

Email redbox@sutechs.com. If you’re reporting a problem, include your device, operating system, the level, and what happened. Please don’t send passwords.

Availability

The web game is available now. Google Play is in preparation. The App Store version is coming soon.
